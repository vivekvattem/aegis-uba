# Behavioural Assumptions

- Each entity is assigned a stable home region, preferred authentication method, known device fingerprints, common resources, common protocols, and habitual hours.
- Users are more daytime-oriented, while service accounts and edge devices may operate around the clock.
- Normal activity is sampled around per-entity medians with moderate stochastic noise.
- Sensitive resources and privileged commands are rarer and therefore more suspicious when deviating from the baseline.
